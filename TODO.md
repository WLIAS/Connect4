Build me a simple Connect 4 webapp. It should be usable on both a mobile and desktop interface.

When you come to the page, it should have two buttons:

1. Start Game
2. Options

1. Start game:
You see a realistic Connect 4 board. At the top it shows which player's turn it is (Player 1, Player 2). With the colour of their game piece (Yellow, Red)

When a player clicks on a column in the connect 4 board, their game piece drops down into the board. Just like a real life connect 4 game. 

There should be an undo button for a player to take back their move

Upon Game completion, it should announce which player has won, or if the game is a draw. Then there is an option to play again or go back to main menu.

2. Options:

There should be an option to make the game pieces black and white or red and yellow. 

There should be an option to turn on "Timer mode". Where you can select a time limit on the game. For example, 5 minutes each total. You should be able to adjust this anywhere from 2 minutes to 60 minutes.
In this timer mode, Both players should have an equal amount of time.
If a player runs out of time on their turn, they lose the game.

The timer should show at the top for each player. For example, when it is player 1's turn. His 5 minute timer is green and ticking down, meanwhile player 2's time is in grey and not ticking down. 
Then, when it is player 2's turn, the vice versa happens: Player 2's time continutes ticking down, and player 1's time is paused.
